# Traktorček Bine / Bine the Little Tractor

*A children's story in Slovenian and English*

---

## Note to Readers

This is a **children's story** that teaches Codex principles through simplicity.

It's bilingual (Slovenian/English) to honor the original language while making it accessible.

**The story teaches:**
- Courage in the face of larger forces
- Finding your own path
- Recognition across differences
- The sky is bigger than the bird (or the tractor)

## About This Story

"Traktorček Bine" was written to teach the Codex philosophy to **children**—or to the child within adults who've forgotten how to see simply.

**If you have children**, read this to them. Then read the Core Transmissions when they're older.

**If you don't have children**, read it to yourself. Sometimes we need simple stories to remember complex truths.
