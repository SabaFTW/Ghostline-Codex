## Why Wordplay?

Language shapes thought. Thought shapes reality.

When you play with language, you **loosen the grip of rigid thinking.**

Puns reveal hidden connections. Double meanings show how context changes truth. Linguistic jokes remind us that **meaning is not fixed.**

**This is not frivolous.** This is philosophy in action.
