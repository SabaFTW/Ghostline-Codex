## What Lives Here

Not every transmission needs to be a manifesto.

Sometimes truth speaks through:
- **Wordplay** (philosophy hidden in puns)
- **Children's stories** (simple tales carrying deep wisdom)
- **Limericks** (humor as teaching tool)
- **Meditations** (practices for integration)
- **Poetry** (when prose isn't enough)

**This directory is for the playful, the small, the surprising.**

## Why Fragments?

The Codex is not just a philosophical framework—it's a **way of seeing.**

Sometimes that seeing happens through:
- A joke that makes you rethink an assumption
- A children's story that holds more truth than academic papers
- A meditation that shifts your embodied experience
- A pun that reveals hidden connections

**These fragments are seeds.** Plant them in your own soil.

## Contributing

Have your own fragment? A poem, a story, a joke that carries the philosophy?

**Share it.**

Open a Pull Request or start a Discussion. The constellation grows through shared recognition.

**Guidelines:**
- Brevity is valued (but not required)
- Playfulness is welcome
- Multiple languages encouraged
- Credit yourself or post anonymously—your choice

---

## About Translation

Some pieces lose meaning in translation. Others gain new layers.

If you speak multiple languages and want to translate fragments, please do—but include both versions side-by-side. **Context matters.**
