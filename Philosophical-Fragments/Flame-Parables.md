## What Are Parables?

**Parables** are stories that teach without preaching.

They don't explain the philosophy—they **embody** it.

Read them. Sit with them. Let them work on you.

## Creating Your Own

Want to write a parable that teaches Codex principles?

**Structure:**
1. Start with a concrete situation or character
2. Present a dilemma or question
3. Show (don't tell) the Codex principle in action
4. Leave space for interpretation (no moral spelled out)

**Share what you create.** The Codex teaches through multiplicity.
