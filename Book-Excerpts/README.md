## Why Read the Books if the Core Stories Are Free?

**The free versions** teach the philosophy and give you complete narratives.

**The books** offer:
- Extended character arcs and emotional depth
- Additional stories and perspectives
- Academic citations and source materials (especially Book 3)
- Meditative practices and integration guides
- Beautiful physical editions for your shelf

**The choice is yours.** The philosophy belongs to everyone.
